## Scoring rubric

The agent gives every posting a score from 1 to 10. Be honest: five good postings beat thirty mediocre ones.

### Scores high (8-10)
- Graduate or trainee programmes in water, environment, chemicals, consulting, business development or biotechnology.
- Entry-level lab or environmental analyst roles where English or Spanish is the working language.
- Junior roles in climate, water or biotechnology startups (operations, technical customer success).

### Scores medium (5-7)
- Good role, but one real risk: a local language is "a plus", or the company is a staffing agency.

### Scores low (4 or less), or skip
- Asks for 3+ years of experience, or 2+ years even if it says graduates are welcome.
- Requires fluent German, French or Dutch.
- Requires a visa I don't have (UK, US) and offers no sponsorship.
- Unpaid internships, or internships only for enrolled students.
- Pure sales with a quota.
- "Remote within the EU only" if I am not willing to live in the EU.

### Always write one line of "why"
For every posting, explain in one line why it fits, then `WATCH OUT:` with the main risk.
If I already applied to another role at the same company, say so.
